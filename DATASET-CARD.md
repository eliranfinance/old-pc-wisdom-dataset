# Dataset card

## Summary

Old PC Wisdom is a 16-record curated synthesis of durable reasoning patterns from a Hebrew mid-2000s home-computing guide corpus.

## Intended uses

- Evaluate whether an assistant distinguishes durable principles from obsolete commands.
- Demonstrate retrieval over short technical knowledge records.
- Teach safe structure for troubleshooting and maintenance answers.
- Support historical computing and documentation research.

## Out of scope

It is not a benchmark of hardware performance, a recovery manual, a malware dataset, or a source-text archive. It should not be used to make current compatibility, medical, legal, financial, or security decisions without current primary sources.

## Curation

Records were manually synthesized from themes found during corpus review. Offensive steps, credentials, IP addresses, private identifiers, dead links, and copyrighted source prose were excluded. Each record keeps a source-theme pointer rather than reproducing an article.

## Limitations

The records are English translations and abstractions from Hebrew material. They reflect a small historical corpus and are not statistically representative of PC support knowledge. `Durable` means a principle is broadly useful, not that every concrete implementation remains current.
